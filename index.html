Privacy Notice: Your registration details are saved securely to YTA's central membership database so that an authorized administrator can review and verify them from any device. Records are protected by admin sign-in — teachers cannot view other teachers' information, and your details are shared with WhatsApp only when you choose to send a recipient message. No unnecessary data is collected, and nothing is sold or handed to outside parties.

YTA Digital Registration System — a tool for collecting Taluka Election membership registrations for Young Teachers Alliance, District Naushahro Feroze. Registrations are stored centrally so any authorized administrator can review and verify them from any device; the registration form still requires an internet connection to save your details, and WhatsApp submission always requires internet.
